### [threathunt.app](https://threathunt.app)

<img width="2018" height="1038" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/951bba1a-061c-45e0-8059-8b5b5ec0322e" />

Threathunt.app is a collection of tools that support internal and external threat hunting operations.

- **Hunt Node**: *Document and track threat hunting operations with node diagrams*
- **Hunt Page**: *Quickly create single page threat hunting reports*
- **Hunt Nexus**: *Graph complex OSINT investigations to evidence findings*
- **Hunt Maturity**: *Threat hunting Maturity Model (HMM) self-assessment tool*
- **Hunt Trace**: *Expose transit paths via Globalping MTR probes to reveal physical ASN relationships*

**Note:** *Some tools are paid. Offers a free 14 day trial for full toolkit access (no payment info needed).*

### [hunt.io](https://hunt.io)

<img width="1831" height="978" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1473e3bf-5fae-4405-9f8d-ea89e9461e3a" />

Hunt.io is a cyber threat intelligence and threat hunting platform that maps and tracks malicious internet infrastructure, such as command-and-control (C2) servers, phishing sites, and rogue IP addresses.

- **Infrastructure Mapping**: *Indexes malicious domains, servers, and services across the global internet*
- **HuntSQL™**: *Allows security teams to run advanced, SQL-based queries on massive threat datasets*
- **AttackCapture™**: *Tags and identifies specific attacker tools and frameworks (like Cobalt Strike)*
- **IOC Enrichment**: *Enhances raw indicators of compromise (IOCs) with network protocols, TLS data, and threat attribution*
- **API & Feeds**: *Delivers real-time data feeds and API integrations for security operations centers (SOCs)*

**Note:** *Tool is paid. Offers a free 14 day trial (no payment info needed).*

*Image from https://portal.hunt.io/*

### [Validin](https://www.validin.com/)

<img width="1917" height="991" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4cf90e17-c6d9-4fc7-bcf3-b6cbb9697e29" />

Validin is a commercial internet intelligence and threat-hunting platform that aggregates historical DNS data, host responses, SSL certificates, and open-source intelligence (OSINT) to help cybersecurity teams track and investigate malicious online infrastructure.

- **DNS History**: *Searches years of passive DNS records and IP resolutions on a single timeline*
- **Pivoting Tools**: *Expands records by A records, nameservers, TLS certificates, and favicon hashes to map threat actor networks*
- **Lookalike Domains**: *Spots newly registered domains that mimic specific target keywords or brand names for phishing detection*
- **API Access**: *Offers developer SDKs and programmatic integration for automated threat enrichment*

**Note:** *Tool is free but does require a "Professional Email" for verification purposes.*
